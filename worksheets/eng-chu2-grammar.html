Part 1. 過去形・過去進行形

【基礎問】次の空所に最も適切なものを選びなさい。
  1. I to the library yesterday.
    (ア) go　(イ) went　(ウ) going　(エ) goes
  2. She not watch TV last night.

    (ア) play　(イ) were playing　(ウ) are playing　(エ) plays
  5. What you doing when I called you?
    (ア) are　(イ) did　(ウ) were　(エ) was
  6. My mother dinner when I came home.
    (ア) cooks　(イ) is cooking　(ウ) was cooking　(エ) cook
  7. We a good time at the party last night.
    (ア) have　(イ) had　(ウ) having　(エ) has
  8. It raining, so we stayed home.
    (ア) is　(イ) was　(ウ) were　(エ) did
【応用問題】
  1. 並べ替え私が電話したとき、彼は本を読んでいました。
    ( a book / he / was / reading ) when I called him.
    → when I called him.
  2. 並べ替えあなたは先週の日曜日どこへ行きましたか。

  4. 書きかえ否定文に。 She was in the library. → She in the library.

  6. 和文英訳彼らは昨日野球をしましたか。 they baseball yesterday?
