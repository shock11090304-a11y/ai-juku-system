Part 1. 関係詞（10問）

  1. The scientist discovery changed medicine won the prize.
    (ア) who　(イ) whom　(ウ) whose　(エ) which
  2. This is the hospital which she was born. （前置詞＋関係代名詞）
    (ア) in　(イ) on　(ウ) at　(エ) of
  3. surprised everyone was his sudden silence. （先行詞を含む関係代名詞）
    (ア) That　(イ) What　(ウ) Which　(エ) It
  4. He has three daughters, all of live abroad. （数量詞＋of＋関係代名詞・非制限）
    (ア) them　(イ) which　(ウ) whom　(エ) whose
  5. Kyoto, I visited last spring, was full of tourists. （非制限用法・目的格）
    (ア) that　(イ) where　(ウ) which　(エ) what
  6. I don't like the way he talks to his staff. （「方法」の関係副詞。the way how は不可）
    (ア) how　(イ) in which　(ウ) what　(エ) by which
  7. You may give this ticket to wants it. （複合関係代名詞）
    (ア) whom　(イ) whoever　(ウ) whomever　(エ) whichever
  8. She is the only student passed the exam. （先行詞に the only。関係代名詞は？）
    (ア) what　(イ) which　(ウ) that　(エ) whose
  9. He said nothing, made us very uneasy. （前の文全体を受ける非制限用法）
    (ア) that　(イ) which　(ウ) what　(エ) it
  10. The manager I spoke yesterday has resigned. （speak to ～。前置詞を意識）
    (ア) who　(イ) that　(ウ) to whom　(エ) whom to

Part 2. 助動詞（6問）

  1. You have told me earlier — I could have helped you then. （過去への非難・後悔）
    (ア) should　(イ) must　(ウ) can　(エ) will
  2. He looks pale. He have caught a cold. （過去の推量「〜したのかもしれない」）
    (ア) may　(イ) should　(ウ) need　(エ) ought
  3. I would you didn't smoke in this room. （would rather＋S＋過去形）
    (ア) better　(イ) rather　(ウ) prefer　(エ) sooner
  4. There be a tall tree here, but it was cut down. （過去の「状態」。would は不可）
    (ア) would　(イ) used to　(ウ) could　(エ) might
  5. It's hopeless. We may well give up now. （may as well「〜したほうがよい／どうせなら」）
    (ア) so　(イ) too　(ウ) as　(エ) very
  6. In the future, robots will do this job for us. （助動詞は重ねられない：will can は不可）
    (ア) can　(イ) be able to　(ウ) could　(エ) able to
